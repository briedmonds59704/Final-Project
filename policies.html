Attendance Policy

Much like other purchases—you will find yourself responsible for the quality of your experience. My responsibility is to many students—I expect that if you want to know what this whole typography thing is about, you're going to show up on time—all the time. If you think this might not be the right time for this class consider doing this another time. If you are found absent more than twice you will be ineligible for an A grade. More than 3 times you would be ineligible for a C.
