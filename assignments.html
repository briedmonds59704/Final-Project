  1. September 14, 2027

    MA 1 - Hand Drawn Letters

    Draw with some accuracy and fidelity the following letters, (ALL LETTERS) each in the sizes stated below (ALL SIZES), in three of the typefaces stated below. (CHOOSE 3.).

    Provide a 2 sentence biography of the designers of any typefaces you choose.

    MAJISCULE: CAPITAL: UPPERCASE:
    B Q K Y
    MINUSCULE: COMMON: LOWERCASE:
    a g p y
    NUMBER: NUMERAL: DIGIT:
    any Numeral you choose
    SIZES IN PTS. and PICAS
    36 72 222 450 PTS / 25 PICAS
    TYPEFACES
    Bodoni Extra Bold Italic
    Trade Gothic Condensed 20 (BOLD)
    Baskerville Italic
    Garamond Roman
    Interstate Gothic
    Futura Extra Bold

    This assignment is to be performed during the recitation, and also where ever the student is most comfortable. During the recitation the LA will help guide research for the student group and general solutions for tools and expectations.
    There are 150 Drawings which can be made into any artifact the student would like, a book, a poster, a stack of uncoordinated papers. Whatever.
    The B grade is getting it done. The A grade makes it interesting.

    Use any tool you wish and only that tool.
    1 color only excluding paper colors

  2. October 5, 2027

    MA 2 - NUMBERS and Statistics

    Pull 1 Statistic from each of the following:

    • Fin Tech - Financial Times (Online or Newspaper. Provide citation)
    • Op Ed. - New York Times (Online or Newspaper. Provide citation)
    • Sports - ESPN (TV, Provide photo citation)

    Produce (3) 4 Slide Decks to sell any aspect of the research. Recreate a branded template for the author of each deck. Each deck is from a different author position. Pro/Con/Neutral.
    This is an assignment at the intersection of speculative futures and storytelling using—more or less—accepted forms of source material and retold and cited for the purposes of promoting a favorable/unfavorable circumstance and the consequences that are in the truth of this 'fact'. Numbers are central to this work. There must be a number story. Use AI at your own risk. AI errors are common, if the AI errors are present in your finished work. Your final grade for the assignment will drop by a full letter grade.
