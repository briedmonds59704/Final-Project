Assignments

Major Assignments

  1. MA ONE DUE 9/14

    Hand Drawn Letters

    Draw with some accuracy and fidelity the specified letters, sizes, and typefaces. Provide a two-sentence biography of the selected designers.

    Letters: B Q K Y, a g p y, and an additional numeral of your choosing.

    Sizes: 36, 72, 222, and 450 points / 25 picas.

    Typefaces: Bodoni Extra Bold Italic, Trade Gothic Condensed 20 (Bold), Baskerville Italic, Garamond Roman, Interstate Gothic, and Futura Extra Bold.

    There are 150 drawings in total, and the assignment can be assembled as a book, poster, or stack of paper. One color only, excluding the paper.

  2. MA TWO DUE 10/5

    Numbers and Statistics

    Pull one statistic from each of the following sources: Financial Times, New York Times, and ESPN. Cite each source properly.

    Produce three four-slide decks that sell an aspect of the research from a different author position: pro, con, or neutral.

    Use numbers as a narrative device and recreate a branded template for each deck. AI-use is allowed but errors will cost a full letter grade.

  3. MA THREE DUE 11/15

    Remake the Syllabus

    Take everything you have learned and redesign this course document for future students. The work should be clear, empathetic, and instructive, with expectations around attendance, grading, and deadlines made explicit.

    Use the university academic calendar for the following semester, and include all of the required course content, major assignments, and schedule details.

    Make a website or a PDF as the final output. Print the PDF, not a webpage. The final document must be editable and can use as many pages as needed.
